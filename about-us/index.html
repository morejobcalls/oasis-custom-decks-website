About us

Service built on Navy values

For Jacob Weaver, founder of Oasis Custom Decks, excellence isn't just a business principle — it's a way of life shaped by years of service in the United States Navy.

The discipline, attention to detail, accountability, and commitment to doing the job right the first time continue to influence every project we undertake. Those same values guide our team on every deck we build, from the initial consultation to the final walkthrough.

We believe in earning our clients' trust through honest communication, dependable workmanship, and results that exceed expectations. Read our reviews — you'll notice homeowners keep using the same words: honest, professional, trustworthy. One reviewer hired us after we told her she didn't need a deck company. That's the standard.

Quality & precision

Oasis Custom Decks is an officially approved TrexPro Platinum contractor — a certification earned, not bought — building with the highest-quality materials including Trex and TimberTech. With 40+ years of combined industry experience on the team, your deck is engineered to endure, not just to pass inspection.

Our mission

To help homeowners get the most value for their money with quality workmanship that never compromises structural safety — giving you a beautiful outdoor space and real peace of mind.
