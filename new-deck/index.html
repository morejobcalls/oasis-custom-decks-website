Before you book

Questions people ask before they book

What does a new deck cost?

As a rough range, new wood decks often start around $12,000 to $25,000, and composite decks are commonly $20,000 to $45,000+. Larger elevated, covered, or custom decks can be $50,000 or more. The design meeting is free, and you get a clear, itemized quote, often the same day.

Will someone actually show up?

You pick the day and time yourself on the next screen, and the confirmation comes to your phone and inbox. Jake’s team calls the day before to reconfirm.

What happens at the design meeting?

We come to you, measure the space, talk through what you want, and give you honest options, including the ones that cost less. Plan on about an hour to an hour and a half. It really helps if everyone involved in the decision is there.

Am I going to get a hard sell?

No pressure, no spam. Just a real estimate from a local, veteran-owned builder. If we are not the right fit for your project, we will tell you.

How long does the build take, and when can you start?

Off-site milling and a disciplined crew mean most projects are completed in days, with a clean jobsite every night. The schedule is locked after the contract is signed and the initial deposit is received.

Wood or composite?

Wood can reduce the upfront price, but the maintenance catches up quickly. Composite is mostly cleaning, and buyers usually see it as a low-maintenance upgrade. We build with Trex and TimberTech and will walk you through the options at the meeting.

Do we need a permit?

Permit requirements depend on the municipality and the exact scope. We will confirm it based on the project and location.

Can you fix or resurface my existing deck?

No. We only take new builds. If you need an existing deck repaired, resurfaced, or rebuilt, we’re not the right fit.
