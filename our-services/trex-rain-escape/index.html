Trex RainEscape

We install Trex RainEscape under-deck drainage systems that keep the area beneath your elevated deck dry, protected, and usable. The system redirects rain, snow melt, and moisture away from the structure so the lower level becomes a true outdoor living space — a dry patio, an outdoor kitchen, or protected storage.

It also protects your deck’s framing from trapped moisture, which extends the life of the whole structure.
